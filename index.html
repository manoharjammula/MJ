Welcome

I am a doctoral researcher at the Multiphase Flow & Microfluidics (MFM) Laboratory under the guidance of Dr. Somasekhara Goud Sontti. My research lies at the intersection of computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and microfluidics, focusing on understanding complex multiphase transport phenomena in microfluidic devices — particularly the generation and deformation of compound (core-shell) droplets.

Research Areas

A closer look at the problems I work on in the MFM Lab, using ANSYS Fluent (VOF / CLSVOF) and high-performance computing.

water_drop

Compound Droplet Generation

CFD simulation of controlled core-shell compound droplet generation in passive microfluidic devices involving complex fluids.

crop_free

Compound Droplet Dynamics

Compound droplet deformation and dynamics while passing through constricted microchannels.

tune

Non-Newtonian Rheology

Microfluidics involving complex fluids is investigated for the generation of single and compound droplets, and the influence of micromixing within the droplets.

cyclone

Mixing within Microreactor

Mixing of components within the shell-protected core microreactor for extraction applications.

insights

Data-Driven & ML-Assisted Modelling

Response-surface and physics-informed neural network (PINN) models built on parametric CFD datasets to predict droplet size, shell thickness, and formation frequency.

biotech

Applications

Findings support controlled microcapsule and double-emulsion synthesis for drug delivery, encapsulation, and lab-on-chip materials processing.
